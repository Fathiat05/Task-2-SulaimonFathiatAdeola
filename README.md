# Decodelabs_Week2_EDA.xslx
**Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Project**
**Project Overview**
This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) using Microsoft Excel. The objective was to analyze retail sales data, uncover trends, identify patterns, and generate actionable business insights.
**Tools Used**
Microsoft Excel
**Analysis Performed**
 Calculated descriptive statistics (Mean, Median, Count)
Analyzed product sales performance
Evaluated revenue generated by each product
Examined customer payment preferences
Assessed referral source effectiveness
Analyzed order status distribution
Reviewed yearly ordering trends
**Key Statistics**
Average Quantity Sold: 2.95
Average Unit Price: 356.41
Average Total Price: 1,053.97
Average Items per Cart: 5.49
Total Records Analyzed: 1,200
**Key Insights**
Chair recorded the highest quantity sold with 562 units.
Printer was the most ordered product with 181 orders.
Online payment was the most frequently used payment method with 258 transactions.
Chair generated the highest revenue, totaling $195,620.11.
Instagram was the top-performing referral source with 259 orders.
Out of 1,200 orders, 250 were cancelled, indicating an area for further investigation.
Order status trends were analyzed to identify changes in customer purchasing behavior over time
**Outcome**
The analysis provided valuable insights into product performance, customer behavior, revenue generation, and operational efficiency, supporting data-driven business decision-making.
